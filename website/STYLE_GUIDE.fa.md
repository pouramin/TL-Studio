# Persian documentation style

Persian copy should sound natural for a technical audience rather than translate every technical term literally.

Prefer keeping established product/developer terms in English when they are clearer, including `Local`, `Workspace`, `Coding Agent`, `Agent`, `Model`, `Session`, `Runtime`, `Provider`, `Prompt`, `Tool`, `Permission`, `CLI`, `Terminal`, `Folder Picker`, `Portable`, `Launcher`, `loopback`, `Server`, and `Reverse Proxy`.

Keep Persian sentence structure and punctuation natural around those terms. Avoid forced equivalents that make the UI or documentation sound translated rather than written in Persian.
